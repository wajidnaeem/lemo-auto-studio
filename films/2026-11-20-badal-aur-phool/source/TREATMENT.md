# Badal aur Phool (The Cloud and the Flower)

A ~54 s kids' cartoon. Look: 1930s rubber-hose cartoon (bendy tube limbs, white four-finger gloves,
pie eyes, things that breathe on the beat, worn-film flicker), **in colour** at the user's request
(early-Technicolor palette instead of the style's usual greyscale). Original characters only.
Dialogue in Hindi/Urdu (Hindustani: everyday words both share), Kokoro offline voices, lip-synced.
English subtitles on period title cards (the user writes in English).

## Three candidate structures
1. **Rescue**: a thirsty flower asks a little cloud for rain; the cloud is too small; together they sing until the cloud swells and it rains. One subject (Badal), one goal (make rain), one turn (friendship makes him big).
2. **Talent show**: garden objects take turns on a stage, the smallest cloud wins with rain. Fun, but many characters and no time for dialogue.
3. **Relay**: a raindrop passed from leaf to leaf across the garden. Lovely music, but no dialogue to lip-sync.

**Chosen: 1.** It has two talking characters (lip-sync), a clear lesson for kids (don't give up, friends help),
and a native move at the peak: the whole garden dances on the beat in the rain, raindrops playing leaves like a xylophone.

## Logline and arc
A little cloud who thinks he is too small to make rain learns, with a thirsty sunflower's help, that singing together makes him big enough.
- Setup: hot day, drooping garden, Phool (sunflower in a red pot) is thirsty.
- Attempt/fail: Badal squeezes with all his might... one tiny drop. "I'm too small."
- Turn: Phool: "Don't give up! Let's sing together!" The band strikes up, Badal swells on every beat.
- Ending: rain on the beat, the garden blooms and dances, a rainbow; iris out on the two friends, Badal winks out of the iris.

## Benchmark (grammar only)
- Early 1930s colour shorts and *Cuphead*: saturated flat character colours over soft painted backgrounds, everything dancing to a hot band. Learn: palette logic, breathing props, music-led gags. Don't take: any character, design, melody, title lettering.

## Cast
- **Badal** (cloud): a round puffy body that *is* the whole character; pie eyes, pink cheeks, rubber-hose arms, white gloves, no legs (floats, bobs on the beat). Voice: hm_psi, light boyish.
- **Phool** (sunflower): yellow petal ring around a brown face, green stem body, leaf-hands with white gloves, red clay pot as "shoes". Voice: hf_alpha.
- **Narrator**: hm_omega, warm storyteller.
- Sun: a hot orange sun with a face, mopping its brow (background gag, no lines).
